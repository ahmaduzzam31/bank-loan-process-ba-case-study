  • Speed is revenue. Every extra week to decision costs approved customers. 27% of applicants withdraw.
  • Fix the front door. A personalised checklist and digital upload target the 41% of incomplete applications.
  • Stop the weekly wait. Delegated authority to €250k and a daily credit forum remove a median 7-day delay.
